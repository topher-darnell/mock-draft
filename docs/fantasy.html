How the scoring works

Standard: 1 point per 10 rushing or receiving yards, 6 per rushing or receiving touchdown, 1 per 25 passing yards, 4 per passing touchdown, minus 2 per interception. Half PPR adds half a point per catch. PPR adds a full point per catch. TE Premium is PPR plus an extra half point for every tight end catch. Fumbles and two-point conversions aren't counted.

Rookie rankings are our own model, not anyone's consensus. They mix where each player goes in our 1,000 simulated drafts (the biggest part), his age on draft day and his college production. Most-likely team comes from those same simulations and will change as the draft order does.
