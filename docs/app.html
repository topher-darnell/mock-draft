On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open bigheadgm.com in Safari. (Chrome on iPhone works too.)

The Big Head GM icon shows up next to your other apps.

On Android

  1. Tap "Install the app" above. That's it.
  2. No button? In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

On a computer: Chrome or Edge shows an install icon at the right of the address bar.
