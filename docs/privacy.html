BIG HEAD GM

Privacy policy

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no selling your data. Effective November 16, 2026. This covers bigheadgm.com and the Big Head GM app for iPhone.

What we collect

  • Visit counts. We use GoatCounter to count page views. It records the page, the site that sent you, your browser and screen size, and your country. It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, and does not follow you to other sites. We use it only to see which pages people like.
  • Your email, only if you sign up for the newsletter. If you type your email into a newsletter box, it goes to Kit, the company that sends our weekly email. We use it only to send that email. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.
  • That's it. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your phone

Your Fantasle and Prospectle streaks and stats, saved mock drafts, your fantasy lineup and settings are saved on your own device (in your browser's or the app's local storage). They are never sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, erases them.

Notifications

The iPhone app can remind you when a new daily Fantasle and Prospectle is ready. The reminder is scheduled on your phone itself, so we don't collect a device ID and there's no notification server. It's off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off anytime in the app or in iPhone Settings.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your information.
  • Show ads or use ad tracking. The app does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Knowingly collect information from children under 13. Big Head GM isn't made for kids.

Links and odds

We link to news stories, stats sites and sportsbook odds reports. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Big Head GM is not a sportsbook and does not take bets.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Questions, or want your newsletter email deleted? Reach us at bigheadgm.support@gmail.com.
