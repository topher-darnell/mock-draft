      How we decide

      • Projection = his points per game (season average with extra weight on his last 3 games) times this week's matchup: how many fantasy points his opponent gives up to his position compared with an average defense.
      • Range is a normal bad week to a normal good week, from how much his scoring has swung this season.
      • Lately compares his last 3 games with the rest of the season. Touches are targets, plus carries for running backs.
      • New quarterbacks (trades, injuries, benchings) move receivers' projections up or down, up to 15%.
      • Injuries come from the official NFL injury report and the latest news: out means 0, doubtful counts a quarter, questionable a little less than normal. Weather and late news aren't counted, so check before kickoff.
