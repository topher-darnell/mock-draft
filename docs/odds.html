What the numbers say

In our simulations, Jeremiah Smith goes first most often (53%). FanDuel's favorite is Jeremiah Smith (+150). On Kalshi, the favorite is Drew Mestemaker (42%). Our simulations always have the Chargers making the pick, and quarterback isn't one of their top needs, so no quarterback goes first in any of our 1,000 drafts. The betting markets are pricing in something our simulations don't: the draft order isn't final, and a team that needs a quarterback could end up with the pick or trade up for it.
