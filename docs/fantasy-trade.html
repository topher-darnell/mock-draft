          How the values work

          • Value runs from 0 to 100. It's how many more points a player should score than a typical starter you could pick up off waivers, in your scoring and league size.
          • Stars are worth more than two average starters added together, and each extra player in a trade counts a little less. You only get so many lineup spots.
          • Redraft uses each player's 2026 points per game, steadied for players who've missed games. Dynasty also weighs age and adds the 2027 rookies and rookie picks.
          • In 1 QB leagues, quarterbacks are worth less than their points suggest, since good ones are easy to find. Superflex and 2 QB change that.
          • Injuries and news aren't counted. Check them before you accept.
