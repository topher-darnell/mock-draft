How we pick

  • We skip the obvious studs and focus on borderline players: the ones people actually ask about.
  • Starts project at least 6% above their usual points per game this week. Sits project at least 8% below theirs.
  • Projections use season scoring with extra weight on the last 3 games, the opponent's fantasy points allowed to that position, injuries and new quarterbacks. A player who's on fire is never a sit, and a player who lost his role is never a start.
  • New picks every Tuesday. Check injury news before kickoff.
