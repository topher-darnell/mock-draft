How it works

  • Matchups: for each defense, we add up the fantasy points it has allowed to each position per game this season and compare it with an average defense. "Easy" gives up about 10% or more above average, "very tough" about 10% or more below.
  • Rank by: "Rest of season" adds up every week left. "Playoffs" counts only Weeks 15–17, when most leagues crown a champ, so players with easy playoff matchups move up. "This week" is just Week 10. "Points per game" ignores the schedule.
  • Projection: a player's points per game (mostly his season average, with extra weight on his last 3 games) times each week's matchup, with 0 on his bye week.
  • New quarterbacks: when a team changes starters (trade, injury, benching), its receivers and tight ends move halfway toward the change in quarterback scoring, and running backs a little, up to 15% either way. Marked "New QB" in the list.
  • Fill-ins: when a player who's been part of his offense lately is out, most of his usual points go to healthy teammates: a running back's mostly to the other backs, a receiver's to receivers and tight ends, split by how much each has been scoring, at 75% (backups are usually less effective). Marked "More work."
  • Injuries: players on injured reserve or out get 0 for the weeks they're expected to miss (marked OUT), from the official NFL roster and injury report plus the latest news. Players out for the season, or not playing with no injury news, are hidden unless you turn them on. Injury news changes fast, so double-check before kickoff.
